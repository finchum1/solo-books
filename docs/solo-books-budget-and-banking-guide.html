Budget & Banking: Quick Start

About 20 minutes to set up,
then about 15 minutes a month

Budget is your plan for the month. Banking brings in your bank statement, matches every line to your ledger, and proves once a month that your books agree with the bank. Solo Books never connects to your bank: you download a statement file from your bank's website and upload it yourself.

Your first session

1Add an accountBanking → Add account

Name it, pick Checking or Savings, and enter its opening balance as of a date: the balance on your bank's statement at the end of the day before your first imported transaction.

2Import a statementAccount → Import statement

On your bank's site choose "Download transactions" or "Export" (CSV, OFX or QFX). Drop the file in. Check the preview, then import. Overlapping files are never double-counted.

3Review the linesAccount → Review tab

Press Match all sure matches. For each line left, choose Add to Ledger (it isn't recorded yet), Transfer (money between your own accounts) or Ignore.

4Reconcile the monthAccount → Reconcile tab

Enter the statement's closing date and ending balance. When the difference is $0.00 and nothing is left to review, press Finish.

5Set a budgetBudget (left menu)

Type a monthly limit beside each expense category and a target beside each income category. Use Copy from last month or Fill blanks from my 3-month average to start fast.

How matching works

SureOne line and one ledger entry with the same amount, close dates, and nobody else competing for them. Safe to match all at once.
PossibleA good guess, but something is unclear (two candidates, or the dates are a few days apart). Look, then confirm.
Group matchTick several lines and the entries they pay for, then match. Use it when one deposit covers several invoices. A difference is allowed, and you can note why.
PayrollNet pay and tax deposits are recognized from your pay stubs, even when one tax deposit covers several runs. A card shows how much of a payroll is still unaccounted for.
TransferBoth sides of a move between your accounts are kept out of income and expenses.

Checking your work

The reconcile screen adds it up for you:

Starting balance$13,045.50
+ this month's lines$1,893.33
Books say$14,938.83

If that equals the statement's ending balance, the difference is $0.00. If it doesn't, look for a missing line, a duplicate, a wrong amount, or a mistyped balance.

Good to know

  • Finishing a month locks its lines so they can't change by accident. Use Reopen on the latest month to fix something.
  • Each month starts from the last one's ending balance. Reconcile in order, one statement at a time.
  • Deleting a ledger entry that was matched sends its bank line back to Review. Undo on a line removes only that line from a group.
  • Wrong columns in the preview? Pick the right ones before importing. An import can be undone until you review its lines.

Reading your Budget

  • Green is on track. Gold means you are close to the limit. Red means you are over.
  • The thin mark on each bar shows where today falls in the month, so you can see if spending is running ahead of the calendar.
  • A category with spending but no limit shows Not budgeted. Type a number to give it one.
  • The budget belongs to one business. All businesses is a view-only roll-up.

Your monthly routine

  1. Download the month's statement from your bank.
  2. Import it, then Review: match all sure matches, handle the rest.
  3. Run payroll as usual. The bank lines it caused are found for you.
  4. Reconcile to the statement's ending balance and finish at $0.00.
  5. Glance at the Budget and pull your Profit & Loss if you need it.
Privacy: only each transaction's date, amount and description are saved. The file itself is never kept. Solo Books never asks for your bank login or full account number, so please keep account numbers out of descriptions and notes too.
